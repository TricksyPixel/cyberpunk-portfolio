Who am I?

A little about myself...

I'm a junior developer based in Perth, Western Australia, with a background that combines graphic design and programming. I originally studied Graphic Design at TAFE, completing both a Certificate IV and Diploma, and continued working on design projects as a side hustle over the years. More recently, I decided to take my career in a new direction and completed a Certificate IV in Programming at South Metropolitan TAFE.

My programming studies gave me a broad foundation across software development, including C#, Java, Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, SQL and software architecture. Since completing my studies, I've continued learning independently, with a particular focus on JavaScript, React and modern front-end development, as well as exploring WordPress and PHP.

I'm particularly interested in front-end development because it brings together two things I enjoy: solving technical problems and creating thoughtful visual experiences. I enjoy taking an idea from a design or concept and turning it into something responsive, accessible and intuitive to use.

While front-end development is where I'd most like to grow my career, my broader programming background means I'm also open to junior software development opportunities. I'm curious, persistent and enjoy learning new technologies and figuring out how things work.
